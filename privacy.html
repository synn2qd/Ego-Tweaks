Privacy Policy

Last updated October 24, 2026

What we collect, why, and what you can do about it.

01Who we are

This policy explains what information Ego Tweaks collects through the egotweaks.com website, the Ego Tweaks app, plan purchases, and our Discord server and bot, and what we do with it. Questions? Open a ticket in our Discord.

02Your account on egotweaks.com

When you make an account we store:

  • your username and email address
  • your password, handled by our account provider Supabase (it's stored securely hashed, and we never see it)
  • when you joined, and your site settings (like whether the intro plays)

When you click to download the app, we record the time, the file and version, and your account if you're signed in. This is how we count downloads.

03Plan purchases

Payments are handled by Payhip. We never see or store your card details. When you buy, Payhip sends us the order ID, your email, what you bought, the price, the payment method type and the date. We use this to check your order and unlock your plan.

Continue with Discord

To download the app or buy a plan, the site asks you to log in with Discord. Discord then tells your browser your Discord user ID, username, display name and avatar, and the list of servers you're in, so the site can check you've joined ours. The server list is only checked in your browser and isn't saved or sent to us. Your browser remembers your Discord name so you don't have to log in every time ("Log out" clears it).

If you're not in our Discord server yet, the site passes your Discord login to our bot, which uses it once to add you to the server (Discord shows this as "Join servers for you" when you log in). The bot doesn't keep it.

Your Discord user ID and username are added to the Payhip checkout, and Payhip passes them back to us with your order. That's how we know which Discord account your plan belongs to, and who our bot should message.

04Discord tickets and the bot

When you use the Ego Tweaks bot (for example to ask for help), we process:

  • your Discord user ID, username and avatar
  • the messages and files you send the bot in your ticket, and our replies
  • which order your ticket is linked to, and when messages were sent

Your messages are shown to our support team in a private staff-only channel in our Discord server. When a ticket is closed, a transcript is saved in a staff-only channel so we have a record of the support we gave. Order details are also saved on the server that runs the bot.

05The Ego Tweaks app

The app reads your hardware and current Windows settings on your PC so it can pick tweaks that fit.

Your hardware details and Windows settings stay on your PC; the app doesn't send them to us.

When you sign in with Discord in the app, Discord tells our server your Discord user ID, username and display name, and the app sends a device ID (a one-way scrambled code made from your Windows installation and Windows user account; it can't be turned back into those details) and its version number. Our server uses these to check which plan you bought, give you your plan's role in our Discord, and keep you signed in. While you're signed in, the app checks in with our server from time to time (at least every 3 days). We keep a record of when you signed in and last checked in, your plan, your device IDs and app versions, and how many app updates were downloaded, so we can count users and downloads. We don't record your IP address in it.

The app also checks our server for updates. Network tests in the app contact public servers (like Cloudflare's) to measure ping.

06Websites and services the site uses

  • The site loads code from jsDelivr (for Supabase) and Payhip (for checkout), and asks Discord for our public member count. Like any web request, these services see your IP address.
  • Your browser saves your sign-in session and, if you used Continue with Discord, your Discord name (local storage), plus a short-lived copy of the Discord member count and your Discord login (session storage). We don't use advertising or tracking cookies.

07How we use it

  • to give you an account and the download
  • to check purchases and unlock your plan in the app
  • to answer support tickets
  • to stop fraud and abuse (for example removing plans from refunded or shared orders)
  • to count downloads and accounts

We don't sell your information, and we don't use it for advertising.

08Who we share it with

Only the services we need to run Ego Tweaks: Supabase (accounts), Payhip (payments), Discord (where tickets happen), and the hosting provider that runs our website and bot. We share information with others only if the law requires it.

09How long we keep it

We keep your account until you ask us to delete it. We keep order details and ticket transcripts as long as we need them for support, refunds and our sales records, then delete them.

10Your choices

You can ask us to see, correct or delete your information at any time by opening a ticket in our Discord. We'll delete your account and the information we hold, except records we have to keep (for example of sales). Payhip and Discord keep their own records under their own privacy policies.

11Children

Ego Tweaks isn't meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them. If you think a child under 13 has given us information, contact us and we'll delete it.

12Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
